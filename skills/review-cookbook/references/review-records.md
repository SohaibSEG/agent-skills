# Review working records

Use one evolving external record in Markdown or the host's existing format. A small review can fit in a single note. Large reviews can split the same fields across linked artifacts. These are working evidence, not mandatory sections in the user-facing report, and do not require adding files to the reviewed repository.

## Target and permissions

Record base/candidate identities, stack dependencies, raw context sources and missing material; review scope and current gate; allowed source/runtime tools and environment boundaries. Record relevant changes to the candidate during review. Artifact identity is not evidence of behavior.

## Path and obligation inventory

| ID / gate / responsibility | Required or preserved outcome; basis | Producers → owner → transformations → effects/consumers | Decisive source evidence | Inspection result / gaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stable local identifier | Requirement, contract or explicit uncertainty | Actual symbols, paths and relevant alternatives | Revision, file/line and what was established | Inspected under conditions / contradicted / unresolved / not reviewed / excluded with reason |

Link source searches to inspected results; do not count files or tokens as coverage. Give shared seams an owner. Separate an unknown business rule from unavailable runtime evidence. A blocked dependent gate is not a design exclusion.

## Candidate and decision record

For each candidate retain its ID, origin/responsibility, gate, rule, supported trigger, causal path, consequence, locations, strongest counterevidence, correction boundary and required proof. Append verification status: accepted, rejected with evidence, or unresolved. Preserve reasons for filtering and links between duplicate manifestations. Keep source conclusions distinct from executed observations.

## Omission audit

Retain the auditor's independent initial inventory, which inputs they had, and the later comparison with discovery. Record newly inspected paths, candidates, resolved disagreements and remaining gaps. If the same agent performs both jobs, label the limitation; do not claim independence through role labels.

## Gate and runtime status

| Gate | Source status | Basis and material gaps | Essential runtime proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design / implementation / experience | Cleared for further review / blocked / not reviewed / not applicable with reason | Inspected obligations, accepted findings or unresolved contract | Verified with command/scenario + revision/environment / outstanding / not applicable with reason |

Source-only assessments can finish their authorized review while readiness stays blocked on essential execution. Execution of one path does not establish another path's runtime guarantee.

## User-facing result

Lead with causal findings and concrete correction recommendations, followed by material coverage and proof limits once. Name the gate reached, review disposition and unperformed essential checks. Link the fuller record when useful; do not paste all internal templates or impose a report-length quota. Review ends at the report, not a fix or delivery loop.
