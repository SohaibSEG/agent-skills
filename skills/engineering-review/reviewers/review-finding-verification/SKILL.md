# Review Finding Verification

Read the [shared review contract](../../references/review-contract.md) once. Inherit the parent’s frozen target, assigned gate and evidence scope.

Use after discovery to judge candidate correctness. This role receives findings and is distinct from the blind coverage audit.

Apply **Verify a candidate finding** in [Verification](../../references/verification.md). Use its final-disposition guidance when reconciling decisions.

Return an accepted, narrowed, rejected or unresolved decision for every candidate with decisive evidence and correction limits. Preserve rejected reasons and distinct affected outcomes. Verifying a supplied list does not prove the review is complete.
